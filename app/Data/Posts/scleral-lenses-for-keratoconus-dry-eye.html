Scleral Lenses over Glasses & Soft Lenses; here’s why

Conditions like keratoconus or severe dry eye aren’t just inconvenient—they impact daily function, emotional well-being, and independence. Glasses often fail to provide sharp vision, and soft lenses may feel unbearable due to constant irritation.

Without proper treatment, symptoms can worsen, making activities such as night driving, reading, or computer use even harder. Scleral lenses offer an advanced, non-surgical option that restores clarity and comfort for patients who previously had limited solutions.

The Specialized Need: Conditions Scleral Lenses Treat

Scleral lenses are custom-made, large-diameter contact lenses that vault over the cornea and rest on the sclera (the white of the eye). This design makes them ideal for:

  • Keratoconus and irregular corneas
  • Post-LASIK complications
  • Post-corneal transplant patients
  • Severe dry eye and ocular surface disease
  • Patients labeled as “hard-to-fit” for traditional lenses

Because they create a smooth optical surface, scleral lenses provide sharp, stable vision that glasses and soft lenses often cannot match.
