The Science of Presbyopia (The “Why”)

Presbyopia occurs when the lens inside your eye becomes less flexible with age. As this flexibility decreases, focusing on objects up close becomes more difficult. Typical symptoms include:

  • Blurry near vision
  • Needing brighter light to read
  •  Headaches after close work
  •  Holding text farther away
  •  Eye fatigue during computer tasks

Multifocal lenses are designed to compensate for this loss of flexibility by providing multiple focusing zones within a single lens.

Multifocal Lenses: The Spectrum of Solutions

Multifocals come in several forms—each providing smooth, comfortable transitions between viewing distances.

Progressive (No-Line) Glasses

  •  Natural visual flow from distance to near
  • No visible bifocal line
  •  Ideal for daily wear and all-purpose use

Bifocal or Trifocal Glasses

  • Defined zones for reading and distance
  • Useful for patients who want clearer separation between powers

Multifocal Contact Lenses

  • Provide near, intermediate, and distance vision
  •  Available in both soft and specialty designs
  •  Great for patients who prefer not to rely on glasses

Whether you need help reading, using a computer, or driving, multifocals offer versatility traditional single-vision lenses can’t match.
