How Ignoring Screen-Related Eye Symptoms Makes Things Worse

Screens force your eyes to work differently than reading print or looking at distance. Over time, that extra effort can lead to accommodative stress, focus fatigue, and chronic discomfort.

Ignoring these symptoms doesn’t make them disappear. Without proper care, digital eye strain can affect productivity, sleep quality, and long-term visual comfort.

A computer vision exam identifies these issues early and helps prevent them from becoming persistent problems.

What to Expect From Computer Vision Exams

A computer vision exam goes beyond a routine eye checkup. It evaluates how your eyes function during prolonged screen use, not just how clearly you see.

What Screen Use Does to Your Eyes

  • Extended screen time often causes:
  • Reduced blink rate, leading to dry eyes after work
  • Vergence dysfunction, making it harder for eyes to work together
  • Accommodative stress, which affects near focusing
  • Blurred vision while reading screens
  • Headaches from computer use and visual fatigue

Blue light exposure and screen flicker can also disrupt circadian rhythm and increase sensitivity to light.

What a Computer Vision Exam Evaluates
