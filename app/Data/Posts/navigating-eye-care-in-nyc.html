How Eye Surgery Works

Eye surgeries fall into a few major categories. Each one requires different planning, technology, and post-operative care — and this is where Austin Optics plays a crucial role.

1. Cataract Surgery (NYC’s Most Common Procedure)

Cataracts cloud the natural lens over time. Surgery replaces it with a new intraocular lens (IOL).

What matters most:

  • Lens choice (standard, toric for astigmatism, multifocal)
  • Corneal mapping to ensure accurate outcomes
  • Laser-assisted vs. manual cataract surgery
  • Pre-surgery dry eye treatment to improve precision

Austin Optics helps patients select the right IOL based on lifestyle, vision goals, astigmatism, and night-driving needs.

2. LASIK & PRK (Laser Vision Correction)

Both reshape the cornea to improve distance vision. LASIK is faster to heal; PRK is better for thinner corneas or certain prescriptions.

We assist with:

  • Pre-op tests (corneal thickness, topography, tear film evaluation)
  • Determining whether LASIK or PRK is safer
  • Managing dry eye symptoms after surgery
  • Fine-tuning post-op visual performance

 

3. Glaucoma Procedures

Glaucoma surgery aims to reduce eye pressure when drops or medications aren’t enough.

Common procedures include:

  • MIGS (minimally invasive glaucoma surgery)
  • Trabeculectomy
  • Tube shunts

Post-surgical monitoring is critical — pressure can fluctuate, healing varies, and early intervention prevents vision loss.

Recovery & Potential Complications

While most surgeries are highly successful, recovery still requires attention. You may experience:

  • Dry eyes
  • Glare or halos
  • Temporary blurry vision
  • Light sensitivity
  • A need for follow-up adjustments

A co-managing eye doctor monitors these changes closely and coordinates with the surgeon if anything needs urgent care.
