Why Patients Choose Austin Optics

Your Eyes Are Not an Accessory

Most optical shops treat eyewear like a retail product. Pick a frame, fill the prescription, move on. Austin Optics does not work that way.

Iris views every pair of glasses as a medical tool. The lens material matters. The coating matters. The fit matters. She has spent 30 years refusing to cut corners on any of it, and her patients notice the difference.

She carries only Crizal and Zeiss lenses because they produce consistently clearer vision and hold up longer than the alternatives. Every frame in the shop is handcrafted in 100% cotton acetate. Nothing in here is generic.

What We Offer

What We Do at Austin Optics

Every service at Austin Optics is handled by a licensed optician with 30 years of experience. You will not be passed between staff members. One person sees you through from start to finish.

Eye & Vision Exams

A full eye health assessment covering visual acuity, glaucoma screening, cataract screening, and retinal health. Not a quick prescription check. A real exam. 

Learn more

Contact Lens Exams

Contact lens fittings require a separate exam from your glasses prescription. We fit all lens types and fill your prescription in 5 to 7 business days.

Learn more

Hard-to-Fit Contacts

Keratoconus, high astigmatism, post-surgical eyes. If another optician has told you contacts will not work for you, that is worth a second opinion. We fit scleral and RGP lenses and have seen most of the difficult cases.

Learn more

Prescription Lenses

We work exclusively with Crizal, Zeiss, and Varilux. Progressive, high-index, anti-reflective, photochromic. Every lens is cut to your prescription. We do not stock cheaper alternatives and we are not going to suggest them. 

Learn more

Computer Vision

If your eyes feel tired by early afternoon and you spend most of your day on a screen, that is a computer vision problem. We fit computer glasses and assess for digital eye strain. There are lens options that help, and most patients notice the difference within a week.

Learn more

Sunglasses & Eyewear

Prescription sunglasses with full UV protection and Crizal coating available. Your eyes take UV damage whether you notice it or not. A good pair of prescription sunglasses is worth the investment.

Learn more

About Iris Alvarez

30 Years. 179,000 Patients. No Shortcuts.

Iris started her optical career on Austin Street in Forest Hills in 1995 under the mentorship of Dr. Fergus. From there, she joined a Park Avenue boutique serving an appointment-only clientele of New York socialites and Fortune 500 executives. She spent 13 years after that at a Brooklyn Heights practice before returning to Forest Hills to co-own Austin Optics in 2016.

In the years since, the practice has grown from 2,500 patients per year to over 9,000. Patients who found Iris in Brooklyn Heights still make the trip to Queens because they have not found anyone better.

She is not interested in volume. She is interested in getting your prescription right.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to make glasses?

Most glasses are ready in 5–7 business days. Contact lens orders also typically arrive within 5–7 business days.

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are preferred so we can give you the time and personal attention you deserve. Walk-ins are always welcome — please keep in mind we may be with another patient.

Is Austin Optics wheelchair accessible?

Yes, our Forest Hills location is fully wheelchair accessible. Public transit and parking are also conveniently available nearby.

Does Austin Optics accept VSP or EyeMed?

Yes — we accept VSP, EyeMed, Davis Vision, Medicare, Cigna, MetLife, and most major vision insurance plans. Call (718) 261-8655 to verify your coverage before your visit.

Can you fill a prescription from my own doctor?

Absolutely. We fill eyeglass and contact lens prescriptions from any licensed doctor. Bring your prescription in or have your doctor’s office send it over.

Do you ship orders?

Yes, we ship with a standard shipping fee. We do not currently offer local delivery.
