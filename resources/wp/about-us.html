How It Started

Twenty-Six Years on Austin Street

Austin Optics was founded in 1999 by Myron Molden and Irene Mercado. They had already been serving the Forest Hills community for over 20 years before opening the practice. By the time they opened their own doors on Austin Street, they already had a patient base that trusted them.

For 17 years, the practice served the neighborhood steadily. When Myron Molden decided to retire in 2016, he sold the practice to Iris Alvarez and Mark, two people who already had deep roots in it.

Mark had worked at Austin Optics from 2001 to 2010 before pursuing other business ventures. Coming back as a co-owner was a return to somewhere he already knew.

Iris took over the clinical side of the practice and rebuilt it from the ground up. Under the original ownership, Austin Optics was seeing approximately 2,500 patients per year. Within a few years of Iris running the practice, that number reached 7,500. Today, it sits at over 9,000 patients per year and continues to grow.

Meet Iris Alvarez

Thirty Years Three Practices One Standard.

Iris began her optical career in 1995 on Austin Street in Forest Hills, under the mentorship of Dr. Fergus, an optometrist who recognized her ability early and encouraged her to pursue private practice. She joined his Park Avenue office shortly after, a sixth-floor, appointment-only boutique that operated with zero foot traffic and a purely referral-based clientele.

The patients there were New York City socialites and CEOs of Fortune 500 companies. The practice was part of a group known as the “Dirty Dozen,” twelve independent optometrists who built successful, insurance-free private practices on reputation alone. For Iris, it was the environment that set her standard. When your patients have every option available to them, and they still choose you, you understand what quality actually means.

In 2003, she joined the practice of Dr. Jordan in Brooklyn Heights after a months-long interview process. Dr. Jordan was looking for someone with a white-glove service background. Iris fits, and she spent nearly 13 years there, seeing 25 or more patients a day, five days a week. Many of those patients followed her when she left.

When she took over Austin Optics in 2016, she brought that standard with her, especially her exclusive work with Crizal and Zeiss lenses. Every frame in the shop is handcrafted in 100% cotton acetate. She also fills prescriptions from any licensed doctor and does not cut corners or stock products she does not believe in.

Over the course of her career, Iris has personally served an estimated 179,000 patients across three practices. That number is the result of showing up every day and doing the work right.

How Iris Thinks About Eyewear

It Is Not an Accessory It Is a Necessity.

Iris says this directly to her patients. Eyewear is not a fashion choice. It is something you put on your face every morning that affects how clearly you see the world for the next 12 to 16 hours.

The lens material matters. The coating matters. The fit matters. A frame that does not fit your face properly will throw off your prescription, no matter how good the lenses are. Iris has spent 30 years learning how all of these things interact, and she applies that knowledge to every patient she sees.

Frames Chosen by Iris Personally

Every frame in the shop is selected by Iris. She carries a curated collection of independent European brands, all handcrafted in 100% cotton acetate. These are not brands you will find at a chain optical.

The current collection includes Silhouette, Face a Face, Anne et Valentin, Lafont, Morel, and Chopard. Each brand was chosen because the materials and construction meet the standard Iris holds for everything in the practice.
